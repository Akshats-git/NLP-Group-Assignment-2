Rate the stories

Rate short stories written by language models

You will read story continuations. Each starts from a given opening and was written by a computer program. You are not told which program wrote which story, and the order is different for every rater.

  • Rate each story from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent) on four questions.
  • Judge only the text on the page. Small models make odd mistakes, so use the whole scale.
  • Rate on your own and don't compare notes until everyone has finished.
  • It takes about 20 to 30 minutes. Your answers are saved in this browser, so you can close the page and come back.

Enter your name so your ratings can be matched to you.
